
# Domain-3 Pilot Overview – Executive Insight / Narrative

## Purpose
Produce high-signal, defensible executive-level insights from AACP traces and control-plane state, without exposing raw logs or low-level events.

## Scope (Pilot)
- Ingest: AACP Audit Sink, Permit decisions, Policy Plane snapshots, Channel Manager state, Registry state.
- Output: Executive Insight Cards + Narrative Briefs + Risk Heatmaps (recommendations only).
- Exposure: Governance Console (Executive Mode) + Scheduled export (PDF/Email integration later, not in pilot).

## Non-Goals
- No operational actuation
- No policy authoring
- No “AI decisioning”
