
# Value & Risk Model (Domain-3)

## Value Hypothesis
- Reduce executive blind spots by translating complex distributed behavior into actionable, trace-backed narratives.
- Shrink time-to-understanding during incidents and governance cycles.
- Improve accountability and audit readiness.

## Success Metrics (Pilot KPIs)
- Executive comprehension score (survey): >= 4/5
- Time-to-brief generation (from data cut): < 60s for reminder briefs
- Trace-backing coverage: 100% of claims must link to TraceIDs
- Hallucination tolerance: 0 (any unbacked statement is a defect)

## Risk Controls
- Evidence-first generation: no narrative claim without explicit evidence pointers (TraceIDs, PolicyIDs, PermitIDs).
- Deterministic templates + constrained summarization.
- Shadow review by Governance before distribution.
