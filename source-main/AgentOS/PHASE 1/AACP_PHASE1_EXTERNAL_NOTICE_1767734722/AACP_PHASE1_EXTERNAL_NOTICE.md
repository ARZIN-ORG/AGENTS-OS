# Phase-1 Status Notice (External – Limited)

Phase-1 of the AACP-based Agent Operating System has been formally closed.

This phase established the governance, control, auditability, and human-in-the-loop guarantees of the platform.
No agent in Phase-1 has autonomous decision or execution authority.

External parties should note:
- All integrations are proposal-only.
- Execution is strictly subject to explicit human approval.
- Governance, audit, and permit flows are non-bypassable.

Further integration opportunities will be evaluated only in Phase-2 under formal governance approval.
